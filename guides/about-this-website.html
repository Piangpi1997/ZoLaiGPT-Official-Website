Website information / ZoLaiGPT

One brand. One independent website.

ZoLaiGPT is a web-based learning and information project focused on multilingual AI literacy. The official website is intended for reading, discovering and exploring — not downloading a separate Android application.

Current scope: This site shares free learning materials, English/Myanmar/Zomi project information, and a scripted browser chat demonstration. It does not provide an Android APK, app source download, paid chat subscription or working checkout.

Our purpose

ZoLaiGPT explores the possibility of AI that better serves people using English, Myanmar (Burmese) and Zomi (Tedim). Accessible technology requires careful language choice, fact checking, thoughtful design and real community feedback. The site's mission is to share ideas and practical learning materials rather than imply that a fully functional AI service is available.

What you can do here

  • Browse free learning guides. Read English-language articles about AI literacy and language-quality checks.
  • Explore the multilingual interface. Use English, Myanmar or Zomi (Tedim) selections for supported labels and descriptions. Some new Zomi content uses English fallback until fluent-speaker review.
  • Try the local chat preview. The homepage and browser workspace demonstrate example chat interactions using scripted replies. They are not connected to a live general-purpose AI model.
  • Follow updates and reach the publisher. Website changes are announced only when confirmed. Visitors can submit correction suggestions or report broken links using the public contact options.

What we do not offer

ZoLaiGPT is currently presented as a standalone website, not a separate app distribution portal. There is no installable APK, Android project download, consumer subscription checkout, Google Play Billing integration or guaranteed live AI service on this public website. Do not rely on screenshots or third-party social posts as proof that such a service exists here.

Language transparency

English is the current source language for longer learning articles. The homepage includes Myanmar content and some Zomi labels; Zomi paragraphs that have not been checked by a fluent Tedim speaker may fall back to English. We should not label untranslated English as native-reviewed Zomi, or silently substitute Myanmar for Zomi.
