Editorial standards and corrections

How this website prepares information, what remains unverified, and how readers can request a correction.

Editorial review needed: This practical article is an AI-assisted draft prepared for the ZoLaiGPT website preview. A human editor must verify accuracy, examples, links and terminology before it is considered published guidance. No advertising or tracking code runs on this page.

Who publishes this content?

These preview pages are prepared for the ZoLaiGPT official website, which also hosts information about Melmuh Tongluan developer source downloads. The site’s public contact is thangdeihpiang21@gmail.com. We do not claim an external editorial certification, a specific professional qualification for the writer, or approval from Google.

How the drafts were created

The current guides were prepared with AI assistance to organize publicly visible project facts and practical steps. They have not yet been independently verified by a human editor. Before any production release, someone responsible for the website should check command examples, links, technical claims, language accuracy, screenshots and the continuing availability of downloadable files. The date a draft was created is not a claim that an older article was freshly researched.

What can readers rely on?

Project-specific factual details such as a file version or checksum should be checked against the project’s current official pages. An article that explains a process is not a product security certification, a legal opinion, a paid endorsement or an app release announcement. If the status changes, the site should update the relevant page clearly rather than quietly rewriting history.

Corrections and feedback

Readers can send an email identifying the page URL and the exact sentence to review. A reviewer should reproduce technical examples, check primary sources and mark the correction with a clear update note if it changes material information. Do not publish invented testimonials, author credentials or fabricated testing results.

Language review

English is the source language for these preview guides. Myanmar (Burmese) and Zomi (Tedim) remain central to the ZoLaiGPT project, but these guides do not claim to have complete or approved translations. Zomi content needs a fluent Tedim speaker’s review; Myanmar and Zomi must never be treated as the same language.

Advertising and independence

No advertising code has been enabled in this preview. Future sponsorships, affiliate links or ads must be clearly distinguished from editorial content, and any required privacy disclosures must be completed first. Editorial guidance should remain useful even if there is no advertising revenue. Readers should not be encouraged to click or interact with ads to support the site.

Privacy and legal status: The website’s Privacy Policy and Terms are still labelled drafts. They require operator and data-practice verification before they can be treated as final notices. Adding an editorial page does not complete that review.

Publication checklist

  1. Confirm facts and file checksums against current project source.
  2. Assign a real responsible editor and approve copy.
  3. Test desktop and mobile accessibility, links and headings.
  4. Review language policy for ad-supported pages and do not place ads on pages primarily using unsupported languages.
  5. Finalize privacy disclosures if advertising is later enabled.
  6. Publish only after separate production approval.
