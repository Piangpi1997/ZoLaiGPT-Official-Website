Editorial standards and corrections

How this website prepares information, what remains unverified, and how readers can request a correction.

How to use this guide: This educational text was prepared with AI assistance and reviewed against the linked reference materials. It is not a software security certification, an approved translation, or a substitute for checking the current official instructions. Report corrections by email.

Who publishes this content?

These educational pages are prepared for the ZoLaiGPT website, independently operated by Thang Piang, with multilingual project information, educational guides and a local chat preview. The site’s public contact is thangdeihpiang21@gmail.com. We do not claim an external editorial certification, a specific professional qualification for the writer, or approval from Google.

How the guides are created

The educational guides were prepared with AI assistance to organize public project facts and technical instructions. Independent, documented human testing has not been completed for every example. Readers should check the linked primary references and independently validate project downloads; the website operator should record any further manual checks and correct issues when found. The date a draft was created is not a claim that an older article was freshly researched.

What can readers rely on?

Project-specific factual details such as a file version or checksum should be checked against the project’s current official pages. An article that explains a process is not a product security certification, a legal opinion, a paid endorsement or an app release announcement. If the status changes, the site should update the relevant page clearly rather than quietly rewriting history.

Corrections and feedback

Readers can send an email identifying the page URL and the exact sentence to review. A reviewer should reproduce technical examples, check primary sources and mark the correction with a clear update note if it changes material information. Do not publish invented testimonials, author credentials or fabricated testing results.

Language review

English is the source language for these guides. Myanmar (Burmese) and Zomi (Tedim) remain central to the ZoLaiGPT project, but these guides do not claim to have complete or approved translations. Zomi content needs a fluent Tedim speaker’s review; Myanmar and Zomi must never be treated as the same language.

Advertising and independence

These learning pages are currently published without Google advertising code. Future sponsorships, affiliate links or ads must be clearly distinguished from editorial content, and any required privacy disclosures must be completed first. Editorial guidance should remain useful even if there is no advertising revenue. Readers should not be encouraged to click or interact with ads to support the site.

Privacy and legal status: The website’s Privacy Policy and Terms are still labelled drafts. They require operator and data-practice verification before they can be treated as final notices. Adding an editorial page does not complete that review.

Publication checklist

  1. Confirm facts and file checksums against current project source.
  2. Assign a real responsible editor and approve copy.
  3. Test desktop and mobile accessibility, links and headings.
  4. Review language policy for ad-supported pages and do not place ads on pages primarily using unsupported languages.
  5. Finalize privacy disclosures if advertising is later enabled.
  6. Make major edits after verification and explicit website-owner approval.

Why accuracy and transparent authorship matter

Google’s people-first content guidance asks publishers to consider who created content, how it was produced and whether readers benefit independently of search traffic. Its AdSense site readiness guidance also emphasizes unique content and usable navigation.

These are general external references, not a finding that ZoLaiGPT meets every approval requirement. An actual editor’s byline, real first-hand testing notes and accurate organizational identity must be provided by the operator, not invented here.
