Website technical update — 25 October 2026

The website stores language and theme preferences as zolaigpt-language and zolaigpt-theme in localStorage, and announcement dismissal as z6-announcement in sessionStorage. Chat demo conversations, notes and text attachments stay in the current tab memory and are cleared on refresh. Local tools process text in the browser. The contact form only prepares a mailto draft in your email app for your review; it does not send or submit a message through this website. Fonts are hosted locally. No live AI provider is connected. This technical description supersedes older conflicting technical descriptions below. The existing legal draft status is retained.

Scope

This policy explains the privacy practices of the ZoLaiGPT informational website. The Android application and any AI services are separate and require their own privacy notices.

Publisher and privacy contact

ZoLaiGPT is the public brand of an independent AI and technology project. The website is operated by Thang Piang as an individual publisher; it is not presented as an incorporated or SSM-registered company. For website questions, privacy requests or corrections, contact thangdeihpiang21@gmail.com. The operator name is displayed for transparency. No residential address, personal phone number, payment profile data, or tax identifiers are published here. Additional legal disclosure obligations still require review.

Information you choose to share

The website displays public contact and social media links. If you email, call, or message through an external platform, you may share your name, contact details, message, and other information voluntarily. The website itself does not currently present an account-registration or message-submission form.

Hosting and technical information

Cloudflare Pages hosts this website. As part of delivering a website, hosting and network providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details, device/browser information, and security logs. Their processing is subject to their applicable practices.

Browser storage, fonts and analytics

The homepage stores your selected interface language in browser localStorage under zolaigpt-language and remembers a dismissed announcement under a zolaigpt-announcement: key. The inspected homepage and legal stylesheets use font files hosted on this website rather than loading a Google Fonts stylesheet. Clearing this site’s browser data will remove these local preferences. The connected Cloudflare Pages project currently has no Web Analytics tag configured in its build settings; this does not rule out Cloudflare security logs, account-level analytics or third-party processing. The publisher must confirm active services, the purposes of processing and retention before approving the final notice.

Google AdSense and advertising

The homepage includes an AdSense ownership-verification meta tag, and this website publishes an ads.txt record identifying its Google advertising account. Neither of these serves ads or means the site has been approved. The inspected website currently has no Google AdSense ad-serving JavaScript. If approved advertising is later enabled, Google and other advertising vendors may place or read cookies and use web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to show, measure or personalize ads. The publisher must update this notice and provide any applicable consent controls before turning ads on. Visitors can manage Google ad personalization through My Ad Center. See also how Google uses information from partner sites.

External services and links

Links to third-party services such as Facebook, GitHub, WhatsApp, Threads, TikTok, Instagram, X, Discord, LinkedIn and email/telephone actions can take you away from this website. Those providers may collect information under their own terms and privacy notices. Visiting the website does not require clicking external download or social links.

Retention and security

Contact correspondence may be retained as needed to respond to inquiries; the specific retention schedule has not yet been confirmed. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may avoid using external social links, clear the stored language preference in your browser, or contact us to ask about personal information you provided. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location and circumstances.

Children

This informational website is not intentionally designed to solicit personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has shared such information.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as website functionality changes. For privacy questions, email thangdeihpiang21@gmail.com.

Cookies၊ Fonts နှင့် Analytics
