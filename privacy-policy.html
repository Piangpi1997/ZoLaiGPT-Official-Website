Scope

This policy explains the privacy practices of the ZoLaiGPT informational website. The Android application and any AI services are separate and require their own privacy notices.

Publisher and privacy contact

ZoLaiGPT is the public brand of an independent AI and technology project. The website is operated by Thang Piang as an individual publisher; it is not presented as an incorporated or SSM-registered company. For website questions, privacy requests or corrections, contact thangdeihpiang21@gmail.com. The operator name is displayed for transparency. No residential address, personal phone number, payment profile data, or tax identifiers are published here. Additional legal disclosure obligations still require review.

Information you choose to share

The website displays public contact and social media links. If you email, call, or message through an external platform, you may share your name, contact details, message, and other information voluntarily. The website itself does not offer account registration and does not collect messages through a server-side form; the preview chat described below accepts input only inside your own browser.

Hosting and technical information

Cloudflare Pages hosts this website. As part of delivering a website, hosting and network providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details, device/browser information, and security logs. Their processing is subject to their applicable practices.

Browser storage, fonts and analytics

The homepage stores your selected interface language in browser localStorage under zolaigpt-language and remembers a dismissed announcement under a zolaigpt-announcement: key. The inspected homepage and legal stylesheets use font files hosted on this website rather than loading a Google Fonts stylesheet. Clearing this site’s browser data will remove these local preferences. The connected Cloudflare Pages project currently has no Web Analytics tag configured in its build settings; this does not rule out Cloudflare security logs, account-level analytics or third-party processing. We keep the services connected to this website under review and will update this notice if the processing described here changes.

Preview chat and workspace demos

The homepage preview chat — and the separate /chat/ workspace demo — run entirely in your browser on this device. Replies are pre-written demonstration content selected locally; they are not live answers from an AI service. Text you type into the preview chat is not transmitted to an AI provider or to a website backend, and chat messages are not intentionally stored in cookies, browser localStorage or sessionStorage, or any database. Messages exist only on the page while it is open and disappear when you refresh or close it. The workspace demo follows the same local model: conversations and any text-file previews stay on your device and are cleared on refresh. Do not type passwords or other sensitive information into the demos; although nothing is sent to us, anything you type is visible on your own screen and device.

Google AdSense and advertising

The homepage and guide pages include the Google AdSense code snippet and an AdSense ownership-verification meta tag, and this website publishes an ads.txt record identifying its Google advertising account. The code is installed so Google can verify and review this site; its presence does not mean the site has been approved by Google AdSense, and the site is not currently serving ads. When the AdSense code loads, Google may process technical information such as your IP address and browser or device details, and may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as verification, security and fraud prevention, as described in Google's own policies; this can happen even while no ads are being served. If the site is approved and advertising is enabled, Google and other advertising vendors may place or read cookies and use web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to show, measure or personalize ads. Any consent controls required for advertising cookies will be provided before advertising is enabled. Visitors can manage Google ad personalization through My Ad Center. See also how Google uses information from partner sites.

External services and links

Links to third-party services such as Facebook, GitHub, WhatsApp, Threads, TikTok, Instagram, X, Discord, LinkedIn and email/telephone actions can take you away from this website. Those providers may collect information under their own terms and privacy notices. Visiting the website does not require clicking external download or social links.

Retention and security

Contact correspondence may be retained as needed to respond to inquiries. The publisher has not set a fixed retention schedule for this correspondence, so no specific period should be assumed. This is different from the preview chat, whose messages stay in your browser and are not retained by the website, and from data processed by Cloudflare and Google, which those providers retain under their own practices. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may avoid using external social links, clear the stored language preference in your browser, or contact us to ask about personal information you provided. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location and circumstances.

Children

This informational website is not intentionally designed to solicit personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has shared such information.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as website functionality changes. For privacy questions, email thangdeihpiang21@gmail.com.
