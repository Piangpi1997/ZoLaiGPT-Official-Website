Questions, answered

Before you begin.

Which languages are in focus?

A product vision that puts English, Myanmar and Zomi (Tedim) front and center.

Is the chat connected to live AI?

No. Both the quick demo and the AI Chat workspace use scripted replies based on existing website content. Messages stay in the current tab and are cleared on refresh.

Can I download a ZoLaiGPT app?

Yes — ZoLaiGPT for Android is available as a beta APK in the Apps directory, with its version, release notes and SHA-256 checksum. Download it only from there.

Who runs ZoLaiGPT?

ZoLaiGPT is an independent project operated by an individual publisher, as described on the About page and in the draft Privacy Policy.
