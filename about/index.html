Good ideas belong in every language.

An independent AI and technology project with a multilingual vision.

A world of possibility, closer to home.

ZOLAI GPT explores how AI tools can become more approachable for people using English, Myanmar and Zomi (Tedim). This website brings together the project’s vision, learning guides and an honest product preview.

We separate working tools from prototypes, keep the interface readable, and explain what happens to your data. The website is operated by Thang Deih Piang.
