ZoLaiGPT V5.5 AI Preview — Data Processing Notice

This notice describes the preview AI chat. It is not a substitute for the website's existing draft Privacy Policy or legal review.

When you send a message

Your chat text and up to eight recent conversation turns may be transmitted to the preview Cloudflare Worker and, when an AI response is needed, to OpenRouter and its selected free-model provider. Matched verified FAQs can be answered directly by the Worker without sending that message to OpenRouter.

Data handling

New Chat clears the current browser conversation only; it does not delete data already processed by Cloudflare, OpenRouter, or model providers. Retention duration, logging, training use and deletion capabilities have not been independently verified. Do not enter passwords, API keys, confidential documents or sensitive personal information.

Language and accuracy

Verified FAQ records are source-backed but are not independent certification of real-world product availability. Zomi (Tedim) translations require native review; the preview uses a labelled English fallback. Free AI models can fail or give incorrect responses.

Scope

This disclosure covers only the V5.5 preview chatbot; the production V5.4 website remains separate. Questions: contact ZoLaiGPT.
